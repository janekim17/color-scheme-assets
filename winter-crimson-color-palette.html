A sharp red in a quiet winter palette

Hushed Blue Gray gives this palette a cool, pale starting point, with Balanced Gray bridging the gap to Deep Charcoal. Focused Red is the clearest warm contrast; Velvet Burgundy gives that red a darker companion. In the previews, charcoal defines the type and upright shape, burgundy forms the round area, and red remains a distinct, concentrated detail.
