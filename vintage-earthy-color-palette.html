Olive depth, softened by dusty pink

Muted Olive and Dark Olive give this palette its earthy depth. Soft Cream opens up the space around them, while Warm Tan adds a golden warmth. Playful Pink changes the olive-and-tan combination with a dusty rose note. In the previews, the pink circle and tan shape remain visible against cream, and the deepest olive gives the smaller action a firm finish.
