Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Color Scheme Studio is operated by the owner of its project repository. Contact for privacy questions: colorschemestudio.contact@gmail.com.

What the website currently collects

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, contact forms, analytics scripts, or advertising code. Its application does not set cookies or use browser storage to track visitors. Fonts, scripts, and image files are served with the site.

Searches and color tools

Palette searches, category filtering, conversions, and comparisons run in your browser. The site does not save them to an application database. The image color picker decodes and samples your selected image locally in your browser; it does not upload or store your photo. Generated palette PNG downloads are created locally. Search terms and category choices may appear in the page URL and browser history. A request for a page can also expose that URL to the hosting provider, so do not enter sensitive information into search.

Hosting requests

GitHub processes technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, which can include IP addresses and request information. Its handling of this data is described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The site’s operator does not run a separate visitor analytics database.

Email and public issue reports

If you email us, your email address, message, and any information you choose to include are available to the site operator and processed through Gmail. They are used to handle the inquiry or correction and any necessary follow-up. Avoid sending sensitive information. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence; any applicable legal retention needs and the email provider’s own handling of data may affect what can be removed.

Reports posted to GitHub’s issue tracker are public and are handled under GitHub’s terms and privacy practices. Use email for an inquiry you do not want to publish. See Google’s Privacy Policy for Gmail’s provider practices.

Clipboard and downloads

When you choose a copy button, it writes the selected HEX value or CSS text to your clipboard. The application does not read clipboard contents. CSS and palette PNG downloads do not require an account.

External websites

Photographer credits and references link to other websites, including Unsplash, W3C, Adobe, and MDN. Those websites have their own data practices when you visit them. A link does not embed their advertising or analytics scripts into this site.

Advertising plans and choices

No advertising code is currently active. We plan to apply for Google AdSense. If Google advertising is enabled, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s advertising cookies allow it and its partners to personalize ads based on those visits.

You can manage Google personalized advertising through My Ad Center and learn more about advertising technologies at Google’s advertising policy information. Relevant consent controls and details of the actual advertising setup will be provided before advertising is activated. This section describes a planned change, not cookies currently set by the site.

Questions and changes

For questions about this policy, write to colorschemestudio.contact@gmail.com. This page will be updated when the site’s actual features or data practices change.
