Soft neutrals with a burnished edge

Pale Cool Gray, Quiet Sage and Warm Taupe share a subdued quality, but each leans toward a different temperature. Burnished Rust introduces an earthy red, while Soft Charcoal gives the neutral colours a firm edge. The previews keep the pale gray around the sage and taupe shapes, with rust in the small action and line details rather than across the whole surface.
