Color Scheme Studio — practical palette editorial template
Updated 2026-10-20 after the owner's direction change.

Purpose: help people turn attractive colors into usable combinations for real work.
Do not describe the photograph or infer a photographer's intention. The photograph is
extraction material only. Keep the full thumbnail and photo-left / palette-right header,
exact five HEX values, title format, category links and bottom photo credit.
Color names describe hue, lightness, saturation or mood, never photographed objects.

Source of truth:
application-plans.mjs — suitable uses, limits, combinations and palette-specific 02/03.
practical-guide.mjs — shared numbered presentation and selected role-sheet controls.
color-roles.mjs — contrast and functional foreground selection.
practical-lessons.mjs — revised Color 101 copy and interactive application examples.
content.mjs maps original immutable metadata through revisePost().
build-studio.mjs applies revised lesson metadata and content. Do not revive legacy
photo-analysis bodies from earlier rose/winter/seasonal modules.

Introduction: identify useful project types and where the palette needs help.
01 — Compare purposeful combinations. Change the field, panel, action, graphics and
foregrounds coherently. Identify any added functional text colors separately.
02 — A palette-specific decision: working subset, format, reading neutral or hierarchy.
03 — A different palette-specific failure and alternative. Add measured pair comparisons
where relevant. A text contrast ratio is not a complete design audit.
04 — Copy/download the currently selected role sheet for non-code use.
No mandatory percentage table or pale-background rule. Dark colors may be broad fields;
pale colors may be emphasis. Omit colors without a useful job. Not every palette suits
long documents, charts, websites and packaging equally. Do not force every use into a post.

Title format: [style or season] + [tone] + color palette.
Examples: Vintage muted color palette / Winter muted color palette.
Keep published URLs stable when an educational title evolves.

Color 101 explains decisions: use, roles, readable pairs, subsets, meaning beyond color,
and output requirements. Keep its educational layout and the approved thumbnail style:
square 1200 x 1200, consistent margins, COLOR 101 label/number, serif headline, simple
topic-specific geometric demonstration, footer rule and studio signature. Default warm
off-white; a dark field may explain a topic. Archive the updated text and topic thumbnail.

Image color picker stays local in the browser. Offer purpose-based role combinations and
explicit additions, not an automatic suitability or accessibility certificate.
Do not submit AdSense review as part of editorial publication.
