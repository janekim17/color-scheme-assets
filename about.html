About Color Scheme Studio

Color Scheme Studio is an independent resource for turning photo-inspired color palettes into practical design decisions. It is maintained by the owner of the Color Scheme Studio project. For questions or corrections, write to colorschemestudio.contact@gmail.com.

What you can use here

Palette posts pair a photograph with five colors, exact HEX and RGB values, and approximate CMYK conversions. Palette pages include a short introduction and small illustrative previews. Color 101 explores colour fundamentals, colour models, perception, digital and print workflows, and practical uses; its articles can stand independently of the palette collection.

How the guides are made

Photographs provide a starting point. We select representative colors, give them descriptive names, and explore how they could work in a layout. The color names are our own labels, not official paint, ink, or standardized color names. Suggested area ratios are design sketches, not measurements of the photograph.

AI-assisted drafting and calculations are part of the production process. The site’s owner directs the project and selects the published material. Examples are original illustrative studies, not client work, real event announcements, or manufactured products. We aim to explain the reasoning and limits of each example rather than present the swatches as a complete design system.

Accuracy and practical limits

Contrast figures describe the exact solid sRGB pairs shown. They do not certify an entire website or text over photography. CMYK numbers use a basic mathematical conversion without an output profile; final printing needs the printer’s specification and a proof when color matters.

Photography and credits

Each palette post ends with the photographer’s name and a link to the original image. Thumbnail graphics combine the selected photograph with the matching swatches. Our Color 101 thumbnails are original topic illustrations. If a credit is incorrect or you have a rights concern, include the page URL when you contact us.

Corrections and funding

Please report unclear explanations, incorrect values, broken tools, or missing credits. We can review the relevant page and correct it. The site currently has no advertising code. We intend to support its continued publication through advertising; any future advertising setup will be described in the Privacy Policy.
