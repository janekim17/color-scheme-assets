About Color Scheme Studio

Color Scheme Studio turns photo-inspired palettes into practical design starting points. Explore a color combination, copy the values, and see how it behaves in a website, a poster, or packaging.

See the colors at work

Our guides connect inspiration with decisions: which color becomes the background, where an accent earns its place, and how to keep text readable. Interactive studies let you compare different balances before you take a palette into your own project.

Make something with it

Each complete guide includes color values, application notes, and useful starter material. Digital contrast checks cover the specific color pairs shown. CMYK conversions are estimates; print results depend on the printer, profile, and paper.
