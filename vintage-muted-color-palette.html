CHOOSE A USE, THEN A COMBINATION

Best for short editorial and campaign layouts

A lively blue-and-red palette for editorial cards, workshop identities, and short announcements. Its two blues are close in value: use them as a family, not as two categories people must distinguish.

Where this palette needs help

For a dense report or dashboard, add a dark reading neutral. Neither blue should automatically become small body text on pink.

02

Two blues do not make two useful chart categories

Use Soft Azure for the main shape and Clear Cyan as a decorative continuation. For a comparison chart, replace one with a distinctly different value or use direct labels and patterns. In a document, a gray reading page can share its palette with a blue cover without sharing its proportions.

03

A pale accent can lead a bold cover

On the blue-led cover, enlarge the pink marker and leave space around it. Its contrast with its surroundings, not its darkness, creates emphasis. On a red-led poster, reduce competing blue and pink decorations so the event name remains first.

04

Take the selected combination with you.

Copy named roles for a document, identity, or web project. The sheet follows the selected use above; it is not a rule to use all five colors everywhere.

Before extending it, test a real heading, a paragraph, a key value, and a secondary detail. Remove colors without a useful role. For printing, request the output profile and inspect a proof; the approximate CMYK values above are not production specifications.
