Cool blues, warm accents

Soft Azure and Clear Cyan bring a cool, clear feel, while Muted Vermilion creates a stronger warm contrast. Gentle Pink softens that shift and Quiet Gray adds a faint green tint. The previews keep the two blue shapes apart so their subtle difference remains visible, with pink in the round shape and red in the smaller action details.
