Contact

Questions about a palette, a Color 101 explanation, or a photographic credit? Contact the Color Scheme Studio operator at colorschemestudio.contact@gmail.com.

Email Color Scheme Studio ↗

What to include

For a correction or tool problem, include the page URL, the color value or section concerned, and what you expected to happen. If a layout problem depends on your device, mention the browser and whether you were using a phone or desktop.

Photo credits and rights

If you are the photographer or rights holder, include the affected page and a link to the original work so we can identify the image. Please do not send identity documents or other sensitive material in an initial message.

Other ways to report a problem

You can also use the public GitHub issue tracker. A GitHub account is required to open an issue. Use email for inquiries you do not want to post publicly.

Before sending

This is a small independent site, and the mailbox is not monitored continuously. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. See the Privacy Policy for how contact messages and site requests are handled.
