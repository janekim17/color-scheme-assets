Deep blues with a quiet warm finish

Deep Teal and Quiet Navy form a dark blue-green range, opening into the lighter Hushed Blue. Pale Lilac offers a faint violet tint instead of a white highlight, while Soft Terracotta is the warm exception. The previews bring lilac into the larger surfaces, with navy, light blue and terracotta shapes above it; deep teal stays in the smaller action details.
