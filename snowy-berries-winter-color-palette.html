Silver, green and a muted warm contrast

Soft Silver gives this palette a cool, pale end, while Muted Sage and Grounded Green build a quiet green range. Muted Coral adds a subdued red contrast and Warm Ochre brings a more golden warmth. The previews put both greens and ochre against silver, leaving coral in the smaller action details. That keeps the combination earthy rather than bright and festive.
